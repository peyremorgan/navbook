Landed in 780c315.

Spec 02 §2.12 reserves the three namespaces and the marker's `plugins` key; spec 04 §4.3 specifies the `nav plugin` verbs, the naming rule, the store and the two loading constraints, and reserves `nav-<name>` on PATH. Spec 03, 05 and 06 follow, and `doc/plugins.md` is the curated page discovery points at.

One departure from the plan, recorded in the commit: **D13 and D14 keep their numbers** rather than becoming `X-kb-1`/`X-kb-2`. The conformance fixtures assert on check codes and every implementation must pass them, so renaming would fork the suite between this implementation and the Rust rewrite, which will have features built in long before it has plugins. The identifiers are grandfathered exactly as `specs/` and `feature:` are. Plugin checks in general are still `X-<short>-<n>`.
